Casa Verde Taytay

A website that brings property information, pricing, availability, and reservation requests into one place for guests.

MY ROLE

Website design & development

PROJECT TYPE

Hospitality & booking

DELIVERY

Live website · 2026
Visit live website ↗

THE GOAL

A clearer digital presence.

Help prospective guests understand the stay, check dates, see pricing, and reach the accommodation through one clear website.

THE APPROACH

Built around the business.

I organized the property information, amenities, guest review link, and booking experience into a responsive website. I also built the date-based pricing calculation and reservation form so guests can review their stay before submitting a request.

KEY FEATURES

  • Property and amenity information
  • Date-based pricing calculator
  • Availability calendar and reservation request form
  • Responsive page layout

WHAT THE WEBSITE MAKES POSSIBLE

From discovering the stay
to planning a visit.

Guests can explore the property, review amenities, select dates, calculate their stay total, and find reservation and contact information in one place.
