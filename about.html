WHO I AM

Business experience.
Practical mindset.

Hi, I’m Jamil — a customer service, administrative and operations professional based in Squamish, British Columbia.

Customer service, administration and operations.

My experience spans customer relations, order coordination, bookkeeping support and day-to-day business operations. At Fisher Coating in Squamish, I worked as Assistant Manager / Lead Hand, coordinating customer orders, shipping and receiving, supplier communications, inventory, purchasing records and production activities.

Earlier, I worked in manufacturing as a Machine Operator at Rogers’ Chocolates and Euramax, following production, quality and safety procedures. I also supported bookkeeping and accounting-cycle activities at R-Vent HVAC Solutions, including organizing transactions, invoices, receipts and expense records, and tracking accounts payable and receivable.

At Tolentino Marketing in the Philippines, I managed daily business operations, sales workflows, customer relationships, purchasing and office administration. I supported bookkeeping, payroll administration, employee scheduling and inventory monitoring, and created the business’s Facebook page for marketing and customer outreach.

I work with QuickBooks Online, Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets and Microsoft Office, and I have completed training in bookkeeping, project management, data analytics and IT support.
