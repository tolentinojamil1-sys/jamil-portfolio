WHO I AM

Hi, I’m Jamil. A website creator based in Squamish, British Columbia.

Good design starts with understanding the business.

My background in business operations, customer service, and project coordination helps me understand what businesses need from their websites.

I’ve built digital projects for hospitality and HVAC businesses, combining practical functionality with thoughtful design. A great website should make your business easier to discover, understand, and contact.

I’m also developing my photo and video editing skills as I continue to explore creative work.

MY UPBRINGING

Business has been part of my life

I grew up in the Philippines around our family businesses: accommodation and apartment rentals, and a sewing machine shop. From around age 16, I helped with customer service, employee coordination, payroll, and day-to-day operations.

That upbringing taught me to take responsibility, listen to people, and understand the work behind a business. Today, I bring that practical perspective into the websites I create.
