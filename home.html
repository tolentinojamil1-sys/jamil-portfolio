BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful answers.

Every project starts with understanding what your business needs.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

I create websites for small businesses, including hospitality and service businesses. My portfolio includes Casa Verde Taytay and R-Vent HVAC Solutions.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. I can help refresh the layout, simplify navigation, update content, or build a clearer path to customer inquiries.

How much will my website cost?

Pricing depends on the pages, features, content, and support you need. Share your project details so we can discuss the scope and a quote.

Do I need my photos and text ready?

Existing photos, business information, and examples you like are helpful. We can identify what is ready and what still needs to be prepared during the first conversation.

What about hosting, domains, and future updates?

We’ll discuss the domain, hosting, and ongoing updates as part of your project scope. Any recurring costs or maintenance arrangements should be agreed before launch.
